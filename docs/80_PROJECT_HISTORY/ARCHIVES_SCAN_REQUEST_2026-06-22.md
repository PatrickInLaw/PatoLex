## 1. Context

PatoLex is building a point-in-time archive of California's statutory law (what a statute
said on any given date), reconstructed from scanned 19th- and 20th-century *Statutes of
California* session-law volumes. For nine chapters across six volumes, the digital scans
we and the public repositories rely on (the Assembly Chief Clerk online archive, and the
mass-digitized HathiTrust / Google Books / Internet Archive copies) **physically dropped
one or two printed leaves at scan time** — the page numbers jump (e.g., printed 1561 → 1564),
so the intervening pages were never digitized by anyone. These are not OCR errors; the
paper pages are simply absent from the digital files. We need **high-resolution scans of
the specific printed pages listed below** from a complete bound set to fill these gaps.

the printed page numbers skip, so the leaf was never digitized by anyone. A scan from a
